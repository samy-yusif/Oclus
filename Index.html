CCPP HOLOGRAM · QUEST 3

2×1 combined cycle plant as a table-top hologram in your room (colour passthrough), controlled with your bare hands.

Checking WebXR…
LEFT pinchgrab the plant: move it and turn it with your wrist
BOTH pinchstretch apart = zoom in, squeeze = zoom out (zooms on the selected equipment; big enough = it opens up)
RIGHT pointthe ray from your index finger (or your fingertip touching a part) shows its data
RIGHT pinch tapselect equipment → part · on empty space = deselect
LEFT palm upwrist menu: Back · Reset · Open/Close · Flows · Data · Tips (poke with right finger)
How to open this on the Quest 3
  1. WebXR only runs from an https:// address, so the file has to be hosted. Easiest: go to app.netlify.com/drop on your PC and drag this HTML file onto the page (rename it index.html first). You get an https link.
  2. Alternative: create a GitHub repository, upload the file as index.html, enable Settings → Pages.
  3. In the Quest 3, open the Browser, type the https link, press Enter AR and allow the permission prompts.
  4. Turn on hand tracking (Settings → Movement tracking → Hand tracking) and put the controllers down. Controllers also work: triggers act as pinches.
  5. Sit or stand in front of a clear table area; the plant appears about 70 cm in front of you. Use Reset in the wrist menu to bring it back.

On a PC this page shows a desktop preview: drag = orbit · right-drag = pan · wheel = zoom (on the selected equipment) · click = select · click empty = deselect.
